Thumb lever

Thumb lever: not connected.

While the walk runs, the lever points to a side the LiDAR measures open beyond 1.65 m at chest height, and rests at neutral for ahead, for unsure and for anything within 0.55 m straight ahead. Open ground with nothing standing behind it counts as unsure, and nothing below 0.6 m is seen. Calibrate with the walk stopped: hold a test button, release for neutral.

Setup

Cloud: off. Questions are answered on the phone.

Height from the ground to the camera, set before Start: chest pocket 1.3, hip 0.9. Side in slow pulses: two short taps for something to the left, one long buzz to the right, one pulse straight ahead; under 1 m the pulse stays plain. Sound under 1 m plays a short tone with each fast pulse, none for slow ones, and with headphones it comes from the side the thing is on; it is off by default, since ears are for traffic. Slow pulses within 2.5 m, fast within 1 m; silence is not clear. Crossings are never judged. This phone asks the cloud once to be approved; after that a question, the photo and the depth go to it, nothing else.
