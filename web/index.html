What this demo can see
Obstacle mode combines YOLO object boxes, relative depth, and appearance-connected floor estimates. Three agreeing frames are required for a cue. Missing models, ambiguity, blur or unsupported geometry returns neutral. Depth has no metric units. Glass, drops, unfamiliar surfaces and hidden obstacles remain unresolved.
Show one large green card inside the lower part of a single camera zone. Two matching frames select that direction. A red card in the same zone, ambiguous targets, or no green target returns neutral. This does not detect safe walkways, distance, stairs, glass, traffic, or people.
Frames are sent sequentially, at most 5 per second. Timing shown here is measured software request time, not physical motion latency. Camera/audio are processed in memory; no recording is saved by the server.
