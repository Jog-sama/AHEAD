Join the Ahead beta

Ahead is an iPhone app for people who walk with a white cane. It reads what is at chest and head height with the phone's LiDAR and tells you through vibration, and it answers a spoken question about a photo. It is in a beta: it has not been walked by many people yet, and it never replaces your cane.

Ahead needs an iPhone with a LiDAR sensor. If you help someone who uses a cane, fill this in for their phone.

Apple sends the TestFlight invitation to this address.

These iPhones have LiDAR: 12 Pro and Pro Max, 13 Pro and Pro Max, 14 Pro and Pro Max, 15 Pro and Pro Max, 16 Pro and Pro Max, 17 Pro and Pro Max. The iPhone Air, the standard models and the SE do not.
